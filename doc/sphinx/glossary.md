    Digital Product Identifier

    Data Spaces Support Center

EDC

    [Electronic Data Interchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_data_interchange)

EI

ERP
    Enterprise Resource Planning

    Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation

FAIR
    Findability Accessibility Interoperability Reuse

Gaia-X

    General Data Protection Regulation

    [International Data Spaces Association](https://internationaldataspaces.org)

    Industrial Digital Twin Association

    International Electrotechnical Commission
